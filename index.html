PDFly Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

Last Updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDFly handles information when you use our PDF tools application. Please read this policy carefully.

1. About PDFly

PDFly provides tools to manage and process PDF documents, including supported features for merging, splitting, compressing, converting, and organizing files.

2. PDF Files and Documents

PDFly's core PDF tools are designed to process selected documents locally on your device. Processing behavior may vary by feature. You should verify important output files and keep backups of important documents.

If any feature sends documents to a remote server or third-party service, this policy must be updated to describe that processing.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

PDFly integrates the Start.io advertising SDK to display advertisements. Depending on the SDK configuration, device settings, and applicable law, Start.io or its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • IP address and advertising identifiers
  • Device, operating system, and network information
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and interaction information
  • Technical information used for measurement, security, and fraud prevention
  • Other information described in the provider's privacy policy

For details about the provider's data practices, please read the Start.io Privacy Policy .

4. Permissions and File Access

PDFly may use Android's document picker and file-saving features to allow you to select documents and save generated files. File access depends on the features used and the permissions granted through Android.

5. Data Storage and Sharing

PDFly's core PDF tools are intended to work on your device. Third-party advertising services may independently process information as described in their privacy policies.

This statement should be updated if the app begins sending data to additional services, servers, or analytics providers.

6. Data Security

We encourage users to keep their devices secure, review app permissions, and maintain backups of important files. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

PDFly is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If the app's intended audience or data practices change, this section must be updated to reflect applicable children's privacy requirements.

8. Your Choices

You can manage files on your device using its file manager. You may also review available advertising and privacy controls through your device settings and the relevant service provider.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, third-party SDKs, or data practices change. The latest version will be made available at this public URL, together with an updated revision date.
